# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales Performance Dashboard

> A Business Intelligence project analyzing the sales performance of a fictional café chain using **Power BI**, with a focus on interactive dashboard design, KPI development, data analysis, and business insights.

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the business performance of a fictional coffee shop chain through an interactive **Power BI dashboard**.

The goal is to transform raw transactional sales data into a clear and actionable business intelligence solution that allows users to monitor sales performance, identify trends, compare stores and products, and better understand customer purchasing patterns.

The project focuses particularly on:

* **Dashboard development**
* **Sales performance analysis**
* **KPI design**
* **Business-oriented data exploration**
* **Data visualization and dashboard UX**
* **Translating data into actionable business insights**

Rather than simply presenting charts, the dashboard is designed to answer practical business questions and allow users to interactively explore the underlying sales data.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to answer key business questions such as:

* How is the café chain performing overall?
* How do sales change over time?
* Which stores generate the most revenue?
* Which products contribute most to sales?
* What are the busiest days and hours?
* Which product categories perform best?
* What purchasing patterns can be observed?
* Where are there opportunities to improve business performance?

## 📊 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of the café chain's performance through a combination of:

* KPI cards
* Time-series visualizations
* Product performance analysis
* Store-level comparisons
* Sales distribution charts
* Category analysis
* Interactive filters and slicers

The dashboard is designed to provide both a **high-level overview** and the ability to drill down into specific aspects of the business.

---

## 🔑 Key Performance Indicators

The dashboard focuses on business KPIs such as:

| KPI                     | Purpose                                  |
| ----------------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Total Sales**         | Measures overall revenue generated       |
| **Total Orders**        | Measures transaction volume              |
| **Total Quantity Sold** | Measures product volume                  |
| **Average Order Value** | Measures average revenue per transaction |
| **Sales by Store**      | Compares performance across locations    |
| **Sales by Product**    | Identifies high-performing products      |
| **Sales by Category**   | Evaluates product-category performance   |
| **Sales by Time**       | Identifies temporal sales patterns       |

> KPI definitions and calculations are implemented within the Power BI data model.

## 🔎 Analysis Areas

### 1. Sales Performance

The dashboard provides an overview of overall sales performance and allows users to examine changes across different periods.

This helps identify:

* Sales growth and decline
* Periods of high and low demand
* Changes in transaction volume
* Overall business trends

### 2. Store Performance

Sales can be compared across different café locations to identify differences in performance.

This analysis helps answer questions such as:

* Which locations generate the highest sales?
* Which locations have relatively lower performance?
* How does sales performance vary across stores?

### 3. Product Performance

The dashboard analyzes sales at the product level to identify products that contribute most to overall revenue and sales volume.

This can support decisions related to:

* Product prioritization
* Menu management
* Promotional campaigns
* Inventory planning

### 4. Product Category Analysis

Products are grouped into categories to provide a broader view of purchasing behavior.

Category-level analysis makes it easier to identify which areas of the menu contribute most to overall business performance.

### 5. Time-Based Analysis

Sales are analyzed across different time dimensions, allowing users to explore:

* Monthly trends
* Daily performance
* Day-of-week patterns
* Hourly purchasing patterns

Understanding these patterns can help businesses optimize staffing, inventory, and promotional timing.

## 🖥️ Dashboard Design

A major focus of this project is **dashboard design and information communication**.

The dashboard was designed with the following principles:

### Clear visual hierarchy

High-level KPIs are placed prominently so users can understand the overall business situation quickly.

### Interactive exploration

Filters and slicers allow users to investigate specific stores, products, categories, and time periods.

### Appropriate visualization

Different chart types are selected according to the analytical question rather than using visualizations purely for decoration.

### Business-oriented storytelling

The dashboard is structured to move from:

```text
Overall Performance
        ↓
Sales Trends
        ↓
Store & Product Performance
        ↓
Customer Purchasing Patterns
        ↓
Business Insights
```
